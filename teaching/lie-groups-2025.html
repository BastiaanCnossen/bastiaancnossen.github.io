Course · Winter 2025/26 · Universität Regensburg

Lie groups and representation theory

An introduction to compact Lie groups and their representations. Among the main results are the Peter–Weyl theorem, which realizes every compact Lie group as a group of matrices, and the Conjugation Theorem on maximal tori, leading to the classification of connected compact Lie groups by their root systems.
