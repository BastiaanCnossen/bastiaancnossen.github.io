Higher Topos Theory · Appendix C

Dense subcategories closed under finite limits

← Previous chapterAppendix B ← Previous sectionSection A.3 → Next sectionSection C.1

The implication (3) \(\Rightarrow\) (4) of Theorem 2.42 requires a presentation of a presentable category \(T\) as a localization of a presheaf category \(\PSh(C)\), where \(C\) is a small category with finite limits and the inclusion \(C \hookrightarrow T\) is left exact. Lurie (2009, Proposition 6.1.5.3) obtains such a presentation from [Lurie 2009, Proposition 5.4.7.4]: if \(T\) is \(\kappa\)-accessible and \(\tau \gg \kappa\) is an uncountable regular cardinal such that \(T^{\kappa}\) is essentially \(\tau\)-small, then the full subcategory \(T^{\tau}\) of \(\tau\)-compact objects is closed under all \(\kappa\)-small limits which exist in \(T\). In particular, \(T^{\tau}\) is closed under finite limits.

For the application, compactness of the objects of \(C\) is irrelevant. What is needed is that the restricted Yoneda functor \(T \to \PSh(C)\) is fully faithful, i.e. that \(C\) is dense. Density, unlike compactness, is inherited by larger full subcategories. It therefore suffices to close a small dense subcategory under finite limits, and this closure remains essentially small. We record this elementary alternative, which avoids the cardinal arithmetic of [Lurie 2009, Section 5.4.7].
