Stable Homotopy Theory and Higher Algebra · Part I · Chapter 8

Ring spectra and modules

← Previous chapterChapter 7 → Next chapterChapter 9 ← Previous sectionSection 7.3 → Next sectionSection 8.1

Cohomology theories are almost never encountered as bare graded abelian groups: they usually carry multiplicative structure. Ordinary cohomology \(H^*(X;\Z )\) comes equipped with a cup product, turning it into a graded ring, and the K-theory of a space (introduced in the next chapter) carries a product coming from the tensor product of vector bundles. Combining Theorem 3.2.6 with the Yoneda lemma, the cup product on \(H^*(X;\Z )\) is induced by a multiplication \(H\Z \otimes H\Z \to H\Z \) on the representing spectrum itself, which is unital, associative and commutative up to homotopy. The resulting structure on \(H\Z \) is that of a homotopy ring spectrum, and the spectrum representing K-theory carries a similar multiplicative structure.

In the \(\infty \)-category \(\Sp \), we can ask for more than a homotopy ring structure: the identifications witnessing unitality and associativity should themselves be part of the data, together with all higher coherences. This leads to the notion of a ring spectrum: a spectrum \(R\) equipped with a multiplication map \(R \otimes R \to R\) which is coherently unital and associative, much like the coherent multiplication of a monoid object in Chapter 5. Alongside it comes the notion of an \(R\)-module: a spectrum equipped with a coherent action of \(R\).

Where ordinary algebra takes place over the base ring \(\Z \), the algebra of ring spectra takes place over the sphere spectrum \(\S \), the monoidal unit for the tensor product on \(\Sp \). As the homotopy groups of \(\S \) are the stable stems, algebra over the sphere is a substantially richer subject than algebra over \(\Z \). Ordinary algebra can be recovered as a fragment of this theory: the Eilenberg–MacLane construction \(A \mapsto HA\) promotes an ordinary ring to a ring spectrum. This also recovers the homological algebra from Chapter 6: for a commutative ring \(R\) we will prove an equivalence \[ \D (R) \simeq \Mod _{HR}(\Sp ), \] identifying the derived \(\infty \)-category of \(R\)-modules with the \(\infty \)-category of \(HR\)-modules. The tools from homological algebra all admit counterparts over an arbitrary ring spectrum.

The homotopy coherent algebra underlying all of this is the theory of \(\infty \)-operads, developed in Part II. Here we take the constructions and properties recorded in Proposition 8.1.5 as given, so that the chapter can be read without familiarity with operads. Results quoted from [Lurie (2017)] are flagged as black boxes where they occur. Supplementary treatments of Ore localization, projective and flat modules, and Tor-amplitude are included in the online version.
