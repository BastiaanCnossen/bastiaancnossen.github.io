Stable homotopy theory and higher algebra · Part III · Chapter 22 · Section 22.4

Tensor products of presentable ∞-categories

In a context of \(\infty \)-categories admitting colimits, one frequently works with bifunctors \(C \times D \to E\) that are bilinear, in the sense that they preserve colimits in both arguments separately. There exists a universal such bifunctor, which is denoted by \(C \times D \to C \otimes _{\colim } D\), and is characterized by the fact that bilinear bifunctors correspond to colimit-preserving functors \(C \otimes _{\colim } D \to E\). The goal of this section is to explain how to turn this into a symmetric monoidal structure on the (very large) \(\infty \)-category of \(\infty \)-categories with colimits. It is convenient to allow for an arbitrary class \(\Kk \) of indexing categories. We specialize to tensor products of presentable \(\infty \)-categories afterwards.

For a collection \(\Kk \) of small \(\infty \)-categories, write \[ \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}} \quad \subseteq \quad \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty } \] for the \(\infty \)-category whose objects are the possibly large \(\infty \)-categories admitting \(I\)-indexed colimits for every \(I\in \Kk \), and whose morphisms preserve all such colimits. More generally, for \(C_1,\ldots ,C_n,D\) admitting these colimits, we say that a functor \[ F\colon C_1\times \cdots \times C_n\longrightarrow D \] \(\Kk \)-multilinear if it preserves \(I\)-indexed colimits in each variable separately for every \(I\in \Kk \).

Lemma 22.4.1. There is a unique suboperad \[ \Oo _{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}} \quad \subseteq \quad \OpCart _{\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }} \] of the cartesian monoidal structure on \(\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }\) whose colors are the objects of \(\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}}\), and its multimorphism are the \(\Kk \)-multilinear functors.

Proof. The two conditions specify a unique subcategory \[ \Oo _{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}}^{\otimes } \quad \subseteq \quad \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\times } \] of the total category of the cartesian monoidal structure, and we need to verify this defines a suboperad. Identity functors preserve the prescribed colimits. Operadic composites of \(\Kk \)-multilinear functors are again \(\Kk \)-multilinear: when one input varies, it occurs in a single inner operation, and preservation of its colimit may first be passed through that operation and then through the relevant variable of the outer operation. The cocartesian lift of a backwards span in the cartesian operad merely reindexes a tuple of colors, and all its component operations are identity functors. It therefore belongs to the suboperad. Its cocartesian universal property restricts to the suboperad because both sides of the relevant mapping-anima equivalence impose the same componentwise \(\Kk \)-multilinearity condition. Finally, the Segal decomposition of a tuple into its individual colors restricts to the suboperad, since \(\Kk \)-multilinearity of a functor with tuple-valued target can be checked componentwise. The characterization in Proposition 14.1.9 now applies. □

The non-formal point is that the multimorphisms in this operad are corepresentable.

Theorem 22.4.2 (Tensor products of categories with prescribed colimits, [Lurie (2017), Corollary 4.8.1.4 and Variant 4.8.1.11]). The \(\infty \)-operad \(\Oo _{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}}\) is the multimorphism operad of a symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category \[ \bigl (\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}},\otimes _{\Kk }\bigr ). \]

Remark 22.4.3. Thus, for every finite collection \(C_1,\ldots ,C_n\) of its objects, there is an object \[ C_1\otimes _{\Kk }\cdots \otimes _{\Kk }C_n \] and a \(\Kk \)-multilinear functor from \(C_1\times \cdots \times C_n\) to this object such that composition induces an equivalence \[ \Fun _{\Kk }\bigl (C_1\otimes _{\Kk }\cdots \otimes _{\Kk }C_n,D\bigr ) \iso \Fun _{\Kk ,\ldots ,\Kk }(C_1\times \cdots \times C_n,D). \] Here the right-hand side is the full subcategory spanned by the \(\Kk \)-multilinear functors. If each \(C_i\) is small, then \(C_1\otimes _{\Kk }\cdots \otimes _{\Kk }C_n\) is small as well.

Suppose first that \(\Kk \) consists of the finite discrete categories. Then \(\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}}\) is the \(\infty \)-category \(\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}}\) of possibly large \(\infty \)-categories with finite coproducts and finite-coproduct-preserving functors. We write the resulting symmetric monoidal structure as \((\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}},\otimes _{\mathrm {coprod}})\); its tensor product is characterized by finite-coproduct-preserving multilinear functors. The full subcategory \(\Cat _{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}}\) on the small colors is closed under this tensor product. In particular, a commutative algebra in \((\Cat _{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}},\otimes _{\mathrm {coprod}})\) is precisely a small symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category whose tensor product preserves finite coproducts separately in each variable.

At the other extreme, let \(\Kk \) consist of all small \(\infty \)-categories, denote the resulting \(\infty \)-category by \(\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {colim}}\), and write \(\otimes \) for this specialization of \(\otimes _{\Kk }\). Its colors are the cocomplete \(\infty \)-categories and its morphisms are the colimit-preserving functors. The full subcategory \(\PrL \subseteq \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {colim}}\) on the presentable colors contains the unit \(\An \) and is closed under the tensor product by [Lurie (2017), Proposition 4.8.1.15]. It is therefore a symmetric monoidal subcategory \[ (\PrL ,\otimes ) \subseteq \bigl (\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {colim}},\otimes \bigr ), \] with tensor product characterized by \[ \FunL (C\otimes D,E) \simeq \Fun ^{\mathrm {L},\mathrm {L}}(C\times D,E). \] This is the monoidal structure used in Section 18.5.
