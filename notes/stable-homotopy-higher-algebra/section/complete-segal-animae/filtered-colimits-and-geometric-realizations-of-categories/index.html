Stable homotopy theory and higher algebra · Part III · Chapter 24 · Section 24.2

Filtered colimits and geometric realizations of categories

The description of \(\infty \)-categories as complete Segal animae allows us to deduce some useful statements about the interaction of finite limits and filtered colimits in \(\Cat _{\infty }\). It will also allow us to clarify the terminology regarding the phrase ‘geometric realization’, which we have used in two different contexts so far.

Lemma 24.2.1. The subcategory \(\CSeg (\An ) \subseteq \sAn \) is closed under limits and filtered colimits.

Proof. Recall that limits and colimits in \(\sAn \) are computed pointwise. The conditions defining the subcategory \(\CSeg (\An ) \subseteq \sAn \) are expressed in terms of finite limits. Since finite limits commute with both limits and filtered colimits1 in \(\An \), the claim follows. □

Corollary 24.2.2. The nerve functor \(N\colon \Cat _{\infty } \hookrightarrow \sAn \) preserves filtered colimits. In particular, finite limits commute with filtered colimits in \(\Cat _{\infty }\).

Proof. The first claim is immediate from Lemma 24.2.1 and the equivalence \(N\colon \Cat _{\infty } \iso \CSeg (\An )\). The second claim follows since limits commute with filtered colimits in \(\An \) and hence in \(\sAn \). □

Corollary 24.2.3. For every finite category \(I\), the functor \(\Hom _{\Cat _\infty }(I,-)\colon \Cat _{\infty } \to \An \) preserves filtered colimits. In particular the core functor \((-)^{\simeq }\colon \Cat _{\infty } \to \An \) preserves filtered colimits.

Proof. By definition, the subcategory \(\Cat ^{\fin }_{\infty } \subseteq \Cat _{\infty }\) is the smallest subcategory closed under pushouts which contains \(\emptyset \), \([0]\) and \([1]\). Since filtered colimits commute with pullbacks in \(\An \), the class \(I\) of \(\infty \)-categories for which the claim holds is closed under pushouts. It thus remains to show the claim for \(I = \emptyset , [0],[1]\). The claim for \(\emptyset \) is clear, since \(\Hom _{\Cat _{\infty }}(\emptyset ,C)\) is terminal for each \(C\) and filtered colimits of terminal objects are terminal. The claim for \([0]\) and \([1]\) follows from the previous lemma, since \(\Hom _{\Cat _{\infty }}([n],-)\) agrees with the composite \(\Cat _{\infty } \xrightarrow {N} \sAn \xrightarrow {\ev _n} \An \).

The last claim is the special case \(I = [0]\). □

Corollary 24.2.4. The \(\infty \)-category \(\Cat _{\infty }\) is compactly generated. Its compact objects are precisely the retracts of finite \(\infty \)-categories.

Proof. Every finite \(\infty \)-category is compact by Corollary 24.2.3. By Proposition 24.1.14, every small \(\infty \)-category is a small colimit of copies of \([0]\) and \([1]\). Writing such a colimit as a filtered colimit of its finite subcolimits expresses every small \(\infty \)-category as a filtered colimit of finite \(\infty \)-categories.

It remains to identify all compact objects. If \(C\) is compact, then applying compactness to a filtered-colimit presentation of \(C\) by finite \(\infty \)-categories shows that \(\id _C\) factors through one of its finite stages. Thus \(C\) is a retract of a finite \(\infty \)-category. Conversely, every such retract is compact. The subcategory of compact objects is therefore the idempotent completion of the small \(\infty \)-category \(\Cat _{\infty }^{\fin }\), and is consequently small. □

Next, we will address the terminology regarding the phrase ‘geometric realization’. Recall that we have previously used this phrase for two a priori unrelated constructions:

  • For an \(\infty \)-category \(C\), the geometric realization \(\geom {C}\) is obtained from \(C\) by inverting all the morphisms in \(C\): \[ \geom {C} := C[W^{-1}], \qquad W := \Map ([1],C); \]
  • For a simplicial anima \(X\colon \simp \catop \to \An \), its geometric realization is its colimit: \[ \abs {X} := \colim _{[n] \in \simp \catop }X_n. \]

The relation between these two notions is explained by Theorem 24.1.10:

Lemma 24.2.5. The geometric realization \(\geom {C}\) of an \(\infty \)-category \(C\) is naturally equivalent to the geometric realization \(\abs {N(C)}\) of its nerve.

Proof. The colimit-functor is by definition a left adjoint to the constant simplicial anima functor: \[ \abs {-}\colon \s \An \rightleftarrows \An \noloc \const . \] For any anima \(X\), the constant simplicial anima \(\const _X\colon \simp \catop \to \An \) is a complete Segal anima (equivalent to the nerve \(N(X)\) of \(X\)), and hence this adjunction restricts to an adjunction \[ \abs {-}\colon \mathrm {CS}(\An ) \rightleftarrows \An \noloc \const . \] Under the equivalence \(\Cat _{\infty } \simeq \mathrm {CS}(\An )\) from Theorem 24.1.10, the constant functor corresponds to the inclusion \(\An \hookrightarrow \Cat _{\infty }\), and by uniqueness of adjoints this implies the claim. □
