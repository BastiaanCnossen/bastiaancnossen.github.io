Stable Homotopy Theory and Higher Algebra · Part II · Chapter 18 · Section 18.1

Tensor products of presentable ∞-categories

← Previous chapterChapter 17 → Next chapterChapter 19 ← Previous sectionSection 17.4 → Next sectionSection 18.2

A functor of two variables that preserves colimits separately should correspond to a colimit-preserving functor out of a tensor product. We now construct such tensor products for presentable \(\infty \)-categories. In fact, we start more generally with \(\infty \)-categories admitting colimits for a prescribed collection \(\Kk \) of \(\infty \)-categories; this will also give tensor products for stable \(\infty \)-categories or \(\infty \)-categories with finite coproducts.

Write \[ \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}} \quad \subseteq \quad \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty } \] for the \(\infty \)-category whose objects are the possibly large \(\infty \)-categories admitting \(I\)-indexed colimits for every \(I\in \Kk \), and whose morphisms preserve all such colimits. More generally, for \(C_1,\ldots ,C_n,D\) admitting these colimits, we say that a functor \[ F\colon C_1\times \cdots \times C_n\longrightarrow D \] is \(\Kk \)-multilinear if it preserves \(I\)-indexed colimits in each variable separately for every \(I\in \Kk \).

The \(\infty \)-categories admitting \(\Kk \)-indexed colimits and the \(\Kk \)-multilinear functors form a suboperad of the cartesian operad \(\OpCart (\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty })\). Indeed, identities preserve the prescribed colimits, and substitution preserves multilinearity because each input occurs in a single inner operation, so Construction 14.1.7 applies.

A symmetric monoidal structure with this multimorphism operad amounts to tensor products corepresenting these multilinear functors.

Theorem 18.1.1 (Tensor products of \(\infty \)-categories with prescribed colimits, [Lurie (2017), Corollary 4.8.1.4 and Variant 4.8.1.11]). The \(\infty \)-operad of \(\Kk \)-multilinear functors is the multimorphism operad of a symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category \[ \bigl (\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\Kk \text {-}\mathrm {colim}},\otimes _{\Kk }\bigr ). \]

Thus, for every finite collection \(C_1,\ldots ,C_n\) of its objects, there is an object \[ C_1\otimes _{\Kk }\cdots \otimes _{\Kk }C_n \] and a \(\Kk \)-multilinear functor from \(C_1\times \cdots \times C_n\) to this object such that composition induces an equivalence \[ \Fun _{\Kk }\bigl (C_1\otimes _{\Kk }\cdots \otimes _{\Kk }C_n,D\bigr ) \iso \Fun _{\Kk ,\ldots ,\Kk }(C_1\times \cdots \times C_n,D). \] Here \(\Fun _{\Kk }\) denotes the full subcategory of functors preserving the prescribed colimits, and the right-hand side is the full subcategory of \(\Kk \)-multilinear functors. The equivalence of \(\infty \)-categories follows by applying the universal property with target \(\Fun (B,D)\) for every small \(\infty \)-category \(B\), since its prescribed colimits are computed pointwise. If \(\Kk \) and each \(C_i\) are small, then \(C_1\otimes _{\Kk }\cdots \otimes _{\Kk }C_n\) is small as well.

Example 18.1.2 (Finite coproducts). When \(\Kk \) consists of the finite discrete \(\infty \)-categories, we obtain the symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category \((\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}},\otimes _{\mathrm {coprod}})\) of possibly large \(\infty \)-categories with finite coproducts and finite-coproduct-preserving functors. Its tensor product classifies functors preserving finite coproducts separately in each variable. The full subcategory \(\Cat _{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}}\) on the small \(\infty \)-categories is closed under this tensor product. In particular, a commutative algebra in \((\Cat _{\infty }^{\mathrm {coprod}},\otimes _{\mathrm {coprod}})\) is precisely a small symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category whose tensor product preserves finite coproducts separately in each variable.

Example 18.1.3 (Finite colimits and stable \(\infty \)-categories). Taking \(\Kk \) to consist of all finite \(\infty \)-categories gives a symmetric monoidal structure \((\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\rex },\otimes _{\rex })\) on the \(\infty \)-category of possibly large \(\infty \)-categories with finite colimits and right exact functors.

If \(C\) is stable and \(D\) admits finite colimits, then \(C\otimes _{\rex }D\) is stable. Indeed, the universal bifunctor sends \((0,d)\) to an initial object. Composing it with \(\id _C\to \const _0\) in the first variable therefore induces a natural transformation \(\id _{C\otimes _{\rex }D}\to \const _0\) by the functor-category universal property. Since its component at the initial object is the identity, this exhibits the functor selecting that object as right adjoint to the functor to \(*\), proving pointedness (Proposition 22.1.2). Since the universal bifunctor preserves finite colimits separately, its universal property identifies suspension with \(\Sigma _C\otimes _{\rex }\id _D\). This is an equivalence, so Theorem 4.2.2 proves stability.

For stable \(C,D\), the tensor product therefore classifies functors \(C\times D\to E\) that are exact separately in each variable, for every stable target \(E\); see Corollary 4.2.27.

Example 18.1.4 (All small colimits). Let \(\Kk \) consist of all small \(\infty \)-categories. We denote the resulting \(\infty \)-category by \(\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {colim}}\) and write \(\otimes \) for this specialization of \(\otimes _{\Kk }\). Its objects are the cocomplete \(\infty \)-categories and its morphisms are the colimit-preserving functors.

For small \(\infty \)-categories \(A\) and \(B\), the external product of presheaves, defined by \((F\boxtimes G)(a,b):=F(a)\times G(b)\), exhibits an equivalence \[ \PSh (A)\otimes \PSh (B)\quad \simeq \quad \PSh (A\times B); \] see Chapterexercise 18.1. The monoidal unit is \(\An =\PSh (*)\).

We extend the presentability assertion to arbitrary presentable \(\infty \)-categories by imposing the relations defining each factor.

Proposition 18.1.5 ([Lurie (2017), Proposition 4.8.1.15]). The tensor product of two presentable \(\infty \)-categories is presentable.

Proof. We may choose presentations \[ C\simeq \PSh (A)^{S\text {-}\mathrm {loc}}, \qquad D\simeq \PSh (B)^{T\text {-}\mathrm {loc}}, \] where \(A,B\) are small \(\infty \)-categories and \(S,T\) are small collections of morphisms, as in Definition 23.2.1. Let \(U\) be the collection of maps \[ s\boxtimes \id _{Y(b)} \quad (s\in S,\ b\in B), \qquad \id _{Y(a)}\boxtimes t \quad (a\in A,\ t\in T) \] in \(\PSh (A\times B)\). By Theorem 23.2.2, the \(\infty \)-category of \(U\)-local presheaves is presentable. We show that it has the universal property of \(C\otimes D\).

Let \(E\) be any cocomplete \(\infty \)-category. By Theorem 23.2.2(4), colimit-preserving functors \(\PSh (A\times B)^{U\text {-}\mathrm {loc}}\to E\) correspond to colimit-preserving functors \(\PSh (A\times B)\to E\) that invert \(U\).

By the presheaf tensor-product formula, colimit-preserving functors \(\PSh (A\times B)\to E\) correspond to functors \(\PSh (A)\times \PSh (B)\to E\) preserving colimits in both variables separately. Inverting \(U\) means that the latter invert \(S\) in the first variable when the second variable is representable, and \(T\) in the second variable when the first variable is representable. Since representables generate under colimits, these conditions hold for arbitrary values of the other variable as well. Applying Theorem 23.2.2(4) in each variable gives an equivalence \[ \Fun ^{\colim }\bigl (\PSh (A\times B)^{U\text {-}\mathrm {loc}},E\bigr ) \simeq \Fun ^{\colim ,\colim }(C\times D,E). \] This identifies \(\PSh (A\times B)^{U\text {-}\mathrm {loc}}\) with \(C\otimes D\), proving the claim. □

The full subcategory \(\PrL \subseteq \widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {colim}}\) on the presentable colors contains the unit \(\An \) and is closed under tensor products. It is therefore a symmetric monoidal subcategory \[ (\PrL ,\otimes ) \quad \subseteq \quad \bigl (\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }^{\mathrm {colim}},\otimes \bigr ), \] with tensor product characterized by \[ \FunL (C\otimes D,E) \simeq \Fun ^{\mathrm {L},\mathrm {L}}(C\times D,E). \]

For presentable \(\infty \)-categories, the tensor product also has the following description. We write \(\Fun ^{\lim }\) for the full subcategory of functors preserving small limits.

Proposition 18.1.6 ([Lurie (2017), Proposition 4.8.1.17]). For presentable \(\infty \)-categories \(C\) and \(D\), there is a canonical equivalence \[ C\otimes D\simeq \Fun ^{\lim }(C\catop ,D). \] If \(L\colon D'\rightleftarrows D\noloc R\) is an adjunction between presentable \(\infty \)-categories, the right adjoint of \(\id _C\otimes L\) corresponds to postcomposition with \(R\).

Proof. The tensor product is computed in the \(\infty \)-category of all cocomplete \(\infty \)-categories. Its universal property therefore applies with target \(\An \catop \), which admits small colimits. Taking opposites and using Corollary 23.2.6 gives \begin {align*} C\otimes D &\simeq \Fun ^{\lim }((C\otimes D)\catop ,\An )\\ &\simeq \Fun ^{\lim ,\lim }(C\catop \times D\catop ,\An )\\ &\simeq \Fun ^{\lim }\bigl (C\catop ,\Fun ^{\lim }(D\catop ,\An )\bigr )\\ &\simeq \Fun ^{\lim }(C\catop ,D). \end {align*}

Here the second line consists of functors preserving small limits in both variables separately, and the third uses pointwise computation of limits. For the last assertion, precomposition with \(\id _C\otimes L\) on representable functors corresponds in the second line to precomposition with \(\id _{C\catop }\times L\catop \). Under representability in the \(D\)-variable, this is postcomposition with \(R\). □

Remark 18.1.7. The suboperad description above identifies presentably symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-categories with commutative algebra objects of \((\PrL ,\otimes )\). Indeed, a commutative algebra object is an operad map \[ \Comm \longrightarrow \Mm _{(\PrL ,\otimes )} \subseteq \OpCart (\widehat {\Cat }_{\infty }). \] Such a map is precisely a symmetric monoidal structure on a presentable \(\infty \)-category whose tensor operations preserve colimits in each variable separately. It suffices to check this for the binary tensor product. Morphisms are colimit-preserving symmetric monoidal functors.
