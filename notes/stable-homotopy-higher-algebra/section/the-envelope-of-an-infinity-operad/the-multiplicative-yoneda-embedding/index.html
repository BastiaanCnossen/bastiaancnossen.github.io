Stable Homotopy Theory and Higher Algebra · Part II · Chapter 19 · Section 19.5

The multiplicative Yoneda embedding

← Previous chapterChapter 18 → Next chapterChapter 20 ← Previous sectionSection 19.4 → Next sectionSection 19.6

The envelope embeds \(\Oo \) into the multimorphism operad of a symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category. The Yoneda embedding of \(\Env (\Oo )\) is symmetric monoidal for Day convolution, so composing gives a fully faithful morphism \[ \Oo \longrightarrow \Mm _{\PSh (\Env (\Oo ))}, \] as in Nikolaus (2016), Proposition 2.7. A color \(y\) is sent to the presheaf represented by its singleton tuple, whose value on a tuple \(\{x_i\}_{i\in I}\) is \(\Oo (\{x_i\}_{i\in I};y)\) by Lemma 19.3.14. We equip the Yoneda functor with a symmetric monoidal structure by using the lax symmetric monoidal structure on the Hom-functor. Whenever necessary, we enlarge the universe so that the envelope is small before forming its presheaf category.

Lemma 19.5.1. Let \(D\) be a symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category. The Hom-functor \(\Hom _D(-,-)\colon D\catop \times D\to \An \) admits a canonical lax symmetric monoidal structure, where \(\An \) carries the cartesian monoidal structure. Its structure maps are given by tensoring morphisms: \[ \prod _{i\in I}\Hom _D(x_i,y_i) \longrightarrow \Hom _D\left (\bigotimes _{i\in I}x_i,\bigotimes _{i\in I}y_i\right ). \] The unit is the point \(\id _{\unit _D}\in \Hom _D(\unit _D,\unit _D)\).

Proof. The twisted arrow construction preserves finite products, by the product formula for hom animae and straightening. It therefore preserves commutative monoid objects of \(\Cat _{\infty }\), giving \(\Tw (D)\) a symmetric monoidal structure. Naturality of the source and target projections makes the left fibration \((s,t)\colon \Tw (D)\to D\catop \times D\) a symmetric monoidal functor.

Under straightening over \(\Span (\Fin )\), the induced functor on total categories \[ (s,t)^{\otimes }\colon \Tw (D)^{\otimes }\longrightarrow (D\catop \times D)^{\otimes } \] corresponds to a natural transformation with components \((s,t)^I\colon \Tw (D)^I\to (D\catop \times D)^I\). These are left fibrations, so Lemma 24.2.2 shows that \((s,t)^{\otimes }\) is a left fibration as well. Its straightening \(H^{\otimes }\colon (D\catop \times D)^{\otimes }\to \An \) sends a tuple \(\{(x_i,y_i)\}_{i\in I}\) to \(\prod _{i\in I}\Hom _D(x_i,y_i)\).

Products in \((D\catop \times D)^{\otimes }\) are given by concatenation. Since \(H^{\otimes }\) sends the projections from a concatenated tuple to the corresponding projections between products of hom animae, it preserves finite products. Its restriction to the singleton fiber is \(\Hom _D(-,-)\), so Theorem 15.3.11 gives the required lax symmetric monoidal structure. The tensor product in \(\Tw (D)\) tensors morphisms of \(D\), and its unit is \(\id _{\unit _D}\), giving the stated structure maps and unit. □

Proposition 19.5.2 (Nikolaus (2016), Proposition 3.9). Let \(D\) be a small symmetric monoidal \(\infty \)-category. Then the Yoneda embedding \[ Y\colon D \to \PSh (D) = \Fun (D\catop ,\An ) \] admits a canonical strong symmetric monoidal structure, where \(\PSh (D)\) is equipped with the Day convolution monoidal structure.

Proof. By the universal property of Day convolution (Definition 16.1.1), a lax symmetric monoidal structure on \(Y\) corresponds to one on the associated functor \(\Hom _D(-,-)\colon D\catop \times D\to \An \). This is provided by Lemma 19.5.1.

It remains to show that this lax structure is strong. Let \(\{d_i\}_{i \in I}\) be a finite collection of objects of \(D\). We must show that the induced map \[ \bigotimes ^{\Day }_{i \in I} Y(d_i) \to Y\left (\bigotimes _{i \in I} d_i\right ) \] is an isomorphism in \(\PSh (D)\). For any presheaf \(H\colon D\catop \to \An \), Proposition 16.1.10 identifies the anima of maps from \(\bigotimes ^{\Day }_{i\in I}Y(d_i)\) to \(H\) with \[ \Nat \left (\prod _{i \in I} Y(d_i)(-), H\left (\bigotimes _{i \in I} -\right )\right ). \] The first functor is the presheaf on \(D^I\) represented by the tuple \(\{d_i\}_{i\in I}\). The Yoneda lemma therefore identifies this anima with \(H(\bigotimes _{i\in I}d_i)\), which is also \(\Nat (Y(\bigotimes _{i\in I}d_i),H)\). Under these identifications, precomposition with the lax structure map is the identity: the tensor product of the identity morphisms of the \(d_i\) is the identity of \(\bigotimes _{i\in I}d_i\). Thus the structure map is an isomorphism. □

Proposition 19.5.3 (Multiplicative Yoneda embedding). Let \(\Oo \) be a small \(\infty \)-operad and put \(E := \Env (\Oo )\). The composite \[ \Oo \xrightarrow {\eta _{\Oo }} \Mm _E \xrightarrow {\Mm _Y} \Mm _{\PSh (E)} \] is fully faithful.

Proof. The map \(\eta _{\Oo }\) is fully faithful by Lemma 19.3.14. Since \(Y\) is fully faithful and symmetric monoidal by Proposition 19.5.2, the induced map \(\Mm _Y\) is fully faithful as well, and hence so is their composite. □
