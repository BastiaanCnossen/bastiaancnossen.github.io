Synthetic Category Theory · Change log

Change log

The following is an overview of the major updates in this book:

  • • September 2026 New chapters on presentable categories.

  • • July 2026: New chapter on K-theory: Waldhausen’s \(S\)-construction and its universal property, and the localization theorem.

  • • June 2026: New chapters on ind-objects and stable categories.

  • • May 2026: New chapter on left exact localizations.

  • • March 2026: Completed part on Peano arithmetic and filtered colimits of animae. Established equivalence between univalent Segal animae and synthetic categories.

  • • February 2026: Completed the parts on straightening/unstraightening, the Yoneda lemma, and functoriality of the opposite category functor. Established full faithfulness of the nerve functor by explicit computations of colimits.

  • • November 2025: Retitled book to Synthetic Category Theory; Rewrote Proposition 3.4.5 to emphasize the symmetry between the equivalent conditions from Proposition 3.4.4; Changed conventions on underlining; Fixed various typos; Removed lots of older material.

  • • April 2025: Redefined \([2]\) as \(\Fun([1],[1])\), as suggested by Christian Sattler. Various reorganizations from Chapter 12 onwards. Changed terminology “fundamental groupoid” to “geometric realization”. Changed convention regarding ‘surjective’ and ‘essentially surjective’, see Definition 6.3.6 and Definition 11.2.3;

  • • November 2024: Started a change log. Revisions and cleaner exposition in Chapters 8, 9 and 12. Reformulation of Axiom B.8 for functor categories;

  • • October 2024: Turned the ‘lecture notes’ into a ‘book project’; Started Section 10.1 on naive set theory within synthetic category theory; Started Section 16.1 on the partially ordered set of natural numbers;

  • • August 2024: Removed axioms on diagram categories, replaced with simpler but more ad hoc approach merely using two synthetic categories \([1]\) and \([2]\);

  • • July 2024: Major rewrite of the introduction;

  • • May 2024: Added Chapter 5 on contexts;

  • • April - July 2024: Incorporated the material from Cisinski’s lecture course Formalization of Higher Categories II during the summer term 2024 in Regensburg: the current Chapters 8, 9, 12 and 10 on universes, cofinality, the Yoneda embedding, peano arithmetic and the simplex category, plus some material which made its way into earlier chapters;

  • • April 2024: Major revision of the lecture notes into its current form: Introduced the language of ‘naive category theory’ in Chapter 1, rewrote the notes in terms of that language. Removed material on tribes, see Part II here;

  • • December 2023: Expanded Chapter 4 with more material on (co)cartesian fibrations;

  • • October 2023 - February 2024: Lecture notes for Cisinski’s lecture course Formalization of Higher Category during the winter term 23/24 in Regensburg. Topics included: the theory of clans and tribes; a discussion of homotopy type theory within a tribe; disjoint unions and functor categories; a definition of simplices via the join construction; a discussion of diagram categories, the Segal axiom and the Rezk axiom; a discussion of groupoids; a (very brief) treatment of (co)cartesian fibrations; subcategories and localizations; the Fundamental Theorem of category theory; universes and directed univalence.
