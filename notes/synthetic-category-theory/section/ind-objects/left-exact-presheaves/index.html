Left exact presheaves

← Previous chapterChapter 17 → Next chapterChapter 19 ← Previous sectionSection 18.1 → Next sectionSection 18.3

Notation 18.2.1. Let \(C\) and \(D\) be two categories. We denote by \(\Lex(C,D)\) the full subcategory of \(\Fun(C,D)\) whose objects are the left exact functors from \(C\) to \(D\). In other words, \[ \Lex(C,D)=\Fun^\kappa(C\catop,D\catop)\catop \] with \(\kappa\) the subuniverse of finite posets.

Proposition 18.2.2. Let \(C\) be a small category with finite colimits. The full embedding \[ \Lex(C\catop,\An)\hookrightarrow\PSh(C) \] commutes with filtered colimits and has a left adjoint.

Proof

The first assertion is a particular case of Lemma 17.2.17 for \(\kappa\) the subuniverse of finite posets. The construction of the left adjoint is a particular case of Construction 17.2.25.
The proposition can also be proved with the small object argument as follows. Let \(h\colon C\to \PSh(C)\) be the Yoneda embedding. If \(\emptyset_C\) denotes the initial object of \(C\), there is a canonical comparison map \[ i\colon\emptyset\to h(\emptyset_C)\, . \] Similarly, given any pushout square in \(C\) of the form there is a canonical comparison map \[ \mathit{comp}\colon h(a')\amalg_{h(a)}h(b)\to h(b')\, . \] This defines a map from the collection of pushout squares of \(C\) into \(\Map([1],\PSh(C))\), the image of which we will denote by \(S'\). We write \(S\) for the union of \(S'\) with the image of the map from \([0]\) to \(\Map([1],\PSh(C))\) corresponding to \(i\) above. We observe the following properties:

  • (a) An object \(F\colon C\catop\to\An\) of \(\PSh(C)\) is \(S\)-local if and only if it is a left exact functor;

  • (b) The anima \(S\) is small and consists of morphisms between compact objects of \(\PSh(C)\).

Indeed, property (a) is a direct translation of the definitions through the Yoneda lemma (under the form of Corollary 12.3.14). The smallness of \(S\) follows from Proposition 11.2.18. The last assertion is a direct consequence of Example 18.1.2 and Lemma 18.1.3.

The proposition is now a particular case of Proposition 18.1.5 and Theorem 18.1.6.

Corollary 18.2.3. If \(C\) is a small category with finite colimits, then the category \(\Lex(C\catop,\An)\) of left exact functors from \(C\catop\) to \(\An\) has small colimits as well as small limits.

Proof

This is a particular case of Corollary 17.2.26.

Proposition 18.2.4. Let \(C\) be a small category with finite colimits. For any object \(x : C^\simeq\), the representable presheaf \(\Hom_C(-,x)\) is a left exact functor from \(C\catop\) to \(\An\). Furthermore, the Yoneda embedding \[ h\colon C\to \Lex(C\catop,\An) \] is a right exact functor.

Proof

This is a particular case of Lemma 17.2.16.

Corollary 18.2.5. If \(C\) is a small category with finite colimits, then, for any object \(x : C^\simeq\), the associated representable presheaf \(h_C(x)=\Hom_C(-,x)\) is a compact object of \(\Lex(C\catop,\An)\).

Proof

For any left exact functor \(F\colon C\catop\to\An\), we have \begin{align*} \Hom_{\Lex(C\catop,\An)}(h_C(x),F) &=\Hom_{\PSh(C)}(h_C(x),F)\\ &=F(x)\, . \end{align*} Since the functor \[ \PSh(C)\to\An\ , \quad F\mapsto F(x) \] commutes with colimits and since the embedding of \(\Lex(C\catop,\An)\) into \(\PSh(C)\) commutes with filtered colimits (see Proposition 18.2.2), this implies that \(h_C(x)\) is a compact object of \(\Lex(C\catop,\An)\).

Definition 18.2.6. Let \(C\) be a category. We let \(\Gamma\) be the collection of all functors of the form \(p\colon X\to C\) where \(X\) is a small filtered category. The category of (small) ind-objects in \(C\) is the full subcategory of the category of presheaves on \(C\) (with values in the category of large animae for a large enough universe) spanned by the image of the map \[ p\colon X\to C\quad\mapsto\quad \underset{x : X}{\colim}\, h_C(p(x))\, . \] The Yoneda embedding of \(C\) induces a full embedding of the form \[ C\hookrightarrow\Ind(C) \, . \]

Remark 18.2.7. Another way to rephrase the definition is \[ \Ind(C)=\Ind_\kappa(C) \] with \(\kappa\) the subuniverse of finite posets. Therefore, a particular case of Corollary 17.2.20 tells us that, for any small category with finite colimits, we have: \[ \Ind(C)=\Lex(C\catop,\An)\, . \]

Theorem 18.2.8. Let \(u\colon C\to D\) be a right exact functor between small categories with finite colimits. Then the adjunction \[ u_!\colon \PSh(C)\rightleftarrows\PSh(D)\colon u^* \] restricts to an adjunction of the form \[ u_!\colon \Lex(C\catop,\An)\rightleftarrows\Lex(D\catop,\An)\colon u^*\, . \]

Proof

This isa particular case of Proposition 17.2.21.

Notation 18.2.9. Given two categories with finite colimits \(C\) and \(D\), we denote by \(\Rex(C,D)\) the full subcategory of \(\Fun(C,D)\) spanned by finite colimit preserving functors.

Theorem 18.2.10. Let \(C\) be a small category with finite colimits. For any category \(D\) with small colimits, precomposing with the Yoneda embedding \[ h_C\colon C\to\Lex(C\catop,\An) \] induces an equivalence of the form \[ \Fun_!(\Lex(C\catop,\An),D)\simeq\Rex(C,D)\, . \]

Proof

This is a particular case of Proposition 17.3.5.
