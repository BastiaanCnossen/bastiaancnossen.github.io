Synthetic Category Theory · Chapter 10

Natural numbers and simplices

← Previous chapterChapter 9 → Next chapterChapter 11 ← Previous sectionSection 9.6 → Next sectionSection 10.1

We construct the natural numbers from the free category on one endomorphism, and then the simplices \([n]\) by repeatedly adjoining a terminal cone point. The resulting simplex category has sifted opposite. Consequently, geometric realization preserves finite products whenever products in the target preserve geometric realizations separately in each variable.
