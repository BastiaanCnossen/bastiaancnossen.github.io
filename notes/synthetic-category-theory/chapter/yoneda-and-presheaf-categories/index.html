Synthetic Category Theory · Chapter 12

Yoneda and presheaf categories

← Previous chapterChapter 11 → Next chapterChapter 13 ← Previous sectionSection 11.6 → Next sectionSection 12.1

The Yoneda lemma identifies morphisms out of a presheaf represented by an object with evaluation at that object; it follows that every locally small category embeds fully faithfully into its presheaf category. A complementary result is Yoneda density: every presheaf can canonically be written as a colimit of representable presheaves. These two results together make arbitrary presheaves accessible through representable ones.

To establish these results in our synthetic setup, we must first construct the opposite category and the Hom-functor. This leads to the twisted arrow category construction, which in turn is used to establish a form of the Yoneda lemma natural in both the representing object and the presheaf.

After having established Yoneda, we collect various important consequences. We first deduce that every category \(C\) is coherently equivalent to its double opposite \((C\catop)\catop\). We then study hom animae of functor categories and the density presentation of a presheaf by its category of elements. Density leads to the free-cocompletion property of presheaf categories and to a version of Yoneda with coefficients. We conclude with small presheaves on categories which need not be small.
