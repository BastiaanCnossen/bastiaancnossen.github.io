Theorem 18.4.7. Let \(C\) be a small category with finite colimits and \(W\hookrightarrow C\) a subcategory closed under pushouts and with the \(2\)-out-of-\(3\) property, with associated localization functor \(\gamma\colon C\to L(C)\). Given a small category \(A\), we denote by \(L(\Fun(A,C))\) the localization of \(\Fun(A,C)\) by the family of natural transformations \(u\colon F\to G\) such that \(u_a\colon F_a\to G_a\) is a map of \(W\) for any object \(a : A^\simeq\). Then, for any finite poset \(P\), the functor \[ \gamma_*\colon\Fun(P,C)\to\Fun(P,L(C)) \] induces an equivalence \[ L(\Fun(P,C))\simeq\Fun(P,L(C))\, . \]

Proof

Recall that any finite poset is finite as a category (Proposition 16.4.23). Let us define \(D=\Fun(P,\Lex(C\catop,\An))\) and let \(S\hookrightarrow\Map([1],D)\) be the family of morphisms in \(\Fun(P,C)\) that are objectwise in \(W\). We see right away that \(S\)-local objects simply are left exact functors from \(L(C)\catop\) to \(\Fun(P,\An)\), hence that \[ D_S=\Fun(P,\Lex(L(C)\catop,\An))\, . \] Since, by virtue of Proposition 18.4.5 the Yoneda embedding \[ h_C\colon C\to\Lex(C\catop,\An) \] induces a full embedding \[ \Fun(P,C)\hookrightarrow D \] taking values in compact objects, Proposition 18.4.2 implies that the functor \[ \loc_S=(\gamma_!)_*\colon D\to D_S \] induces an equivalence \[ L(\Fun(P,C))=\Fun(P,C)_S \] where \(\Fun(P,C)_S\) is the full subcategory of \(\Fun(P,\Lex(L(C)\catop,\An))\) spanned by the essential image of the functor \[ \Fun(P,C)\xrightarrow{ \gamma_*}\Fun(P,L(C))\hookrightarrow D_S\, . \] This implies that the induced functor \[ L(\Fun(P,C))\to \Fun(P,L(C)) \] is right exact and fully faithful (right exactness was already known by Corollary 17.1.8 anyway). For any \(a : P^\simeq\) and any object \(x\) of \(C\), we see that the formation of \(a\otimes x\) commutes with \(\gamma\) by right exactness. Lemma 18.4.6 thus implies that the comparison functor is essentially surjective: its essential image is stable under finite colimits and contains all objects of the form \(a\otimes x\), hence everything.
