Proposition 18.2.2. Let \(C\) be a small category with finite colimits. The full embedding \[ \Lex(C\catop,\An)\hookrightarrow\PSh(C) \] commutes with filtered colimits and has a left adjoint.

Proof

The first assertion is a particular case of Lemma 17.2.17 for \(\kappa\) the subuniverse of finite posets. The construction of the left adjoint is a particular case of Construction 17.2.25.
The proposition can also be proved with the small object argument as follows. Let \(h\colon C\to \PSh(C)\) be the Yoneda embedding. If \(\emptyset_C\) denotes the initial object of \(C\), there is a canonical comparison map \[ i\colon\emptyset\to h(\emptyset_C)\, . \] Similarly, given any pushout square in \(C\) of the form there is a canonical comparison map \[ \mathit{comp}\colon h(a')\amalg_{h(a)}h(b)\to h(b')\, . \] This defines a map from the collection of pushout squares of \(C\) into \(\Map([1],\PSh(C))\), the image of which we will denote by \(S'\). We write \(S\) for the union of \(S'\) with the image of the map from \([0]\) to \(\Map([1],\PSh(C))\) corresponding to \(i\) above. We observe the following properties:

  • (a) An object \(F\colon C\catop\to\An\) of \(\PSh(C)\) is \(S\)-local if and only if it is a left exact functor;

  • (b) The anima \(S\) is small and consists of morphisms between compact objects of \(\PSh(C)\).

Indeed, property (a) is a direct translation of the definitions through the Yoneda lemma (under the form of Corollary 12.3.14). The smallness of \(S\) follows from Proposition 11.2.18. The last assertion is a direct consequence of Example 18.1.2 and Lemma 18.1.3.

The proposition is now a particular case of Proposition 18.1.5 and Theorem 18.1.6.
