Theorem 20.8.3 (Thomason-Trobaugh). Let \(B\) be a small stable category and \(A\) a thick subcategory of \(B\). Then the pullback square induces a fiber sequence in \(\An_\bullet\).

Proof

We let \(C\) be the Waldhausen category defined by \(C=B\) as categories, with cofibrations being all maps but with weak equivalences being the maps whose cofiber lies in \(A\). We observe then that the equivalence \[ \Scons_{n+1}(B)=B^{[n]} \] induces an equivalence of the form \[ \Scons_n(f\colon A\to B)=w_n(C)\, . \] Applying Proposition 20.8.2 to the admissible class of Waldhausen categories given by small stable categories, with \(E=K\), and taking into account Remark 20.7.9, we see that there is a canonical fiber sequence of the form below. It suffices now to see that the morphism of Waldhausen categories \(C\to B/A\) induced by the functor \(B\to B/A\) yields an equivalence of the form \[ K(C)=K(B/A)\, . \] We want to understand the map \[ {\underset{[m] : \Delta\catop}{\colim}\, } {\underset{[n] : \Delta\catop}{\colim}\, } w_m(\Scons_{n}(C))^\simeq\to {\underset{[m] : \Delta\catop}{\colim}\, } {\underset{[n] : \Delta\catop}{\colim}\, } \Iso_m(\Scons_{n}(B/A)) \] where \(\Iso_n=\Fun([n],(-)^\simeq)\). The assignment \[ [m]\mapsto w_m(\Scons_n(C))^\simeq \] is the nerve of the category of weak equivalences \(w(\Scons_n(C))\), whereas \[ [m]\mapsto \Fun([m],\Scons_{n}(B/A)^\simeq) \] is the nerve of the anima \(\Scons_{n}(B/A)^\simeq\). Taking the colimit in the variable \([m]\) amounts to consider the map \[ \Pi_\infty(w(\Scons_n(C)))\to \Scons_{n}(B/A)^\simeq\, . \] It suffices to check that the latter is an equivalence for all \(n\). The case \(n=0\) is obvious (we are comparing two terminal categories). For \(n>0\), identifying \(\Scons_n\) with \((-)^{[n-1]}\), taking into account Theorem 18.4.7 with \(P=[n-1]\), this is a particular case of Proposition 17.1.14.
