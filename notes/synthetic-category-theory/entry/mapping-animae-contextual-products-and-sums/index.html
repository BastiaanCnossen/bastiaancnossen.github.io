Proposition 5.2.4 (Mapping animae for dependent products and sums). For absolute categories \(T,D\) and a category \(B\) in context \(A\), evaluation and restriction along the pair functor induce equivalences of absolute animae \[ \Map(T,\Pi_AB)\simeq\Pi_A\Map_A(W_AT,B), \qquad \Map(\Sigma_AB,D)\simeq\Pi_A\Map_A(B,W_AD). \]

Proof

For the first map, weaken the evaluation functor \(\Map(T,\Pi_AB)\times T\to\Pi_AB\) and compose with \(\varepsilon_B\) to obtain a functor \(W_A\Map(T,\Pi_AB) \times W_AT \to B\). Currying first in the variable \(W_AT\) and then in the \(A\)-variable produces the functor. Conversely, if we denote \(Q := \Pi_A\Map_A(W_AT,B)\), then evaluation provides a functor \(W_A(Q \times T) \simeq W_AQ\times W_AT\to B\). Currying along \(A\) gives \(Q\times T\to\Pi_AB\), which in turn curries to \(Q\to\Map(T,\Pi_AB)\).

For the second map, we weaken the evaluation functor \(\Map(\Sigma_AB,D)\times\Sigma_AB\to D\) and restrict its second input along \(\eta_B\) to obtain \(W_A\Map(\Sigma_AB,D) \times B \to W_AD\). Currying in \(B\) and then along \(A\) produces the functor. For its inverse, write \(Q := \Pi_A\Map_A(B,W_AD)\). Evaluation provides a functor \(W_AQ\times B\longrightarrow W_AD\). Currying in \(W_AQ\) and using preservation of functor categories gives \[ B\longrightarrow\Fun_A(W_AQ,W_AD)\simeq W_A\Fun(Q,D), \] and the axiom for dependent sums extends this to \(\Sigma_AB\to\Fun(Q,D)\). Uncurrying gives \(\Sigma_AB \times Q \to D\), and then currying in \(\Sigma_AB\), with anima parameter \(Q\), gives \(Q\to\Map(\Sigma_AB,D)\).

In each case, the computation identifications for these successive currying operations identify the two composites with the identity after evaluation, which suffices to obtain the identifications at the level of the displayed mapping animae.
