Corollary 14.3.7 (Pointwise localization by fractions). Let \(p\colon W(x)\to C_{/x}\) be a right calculus of fractions at an object \(x\). We consider the adjunction on presheaf categories \[ \gamma_!\colon\PSh(C)\rightleftarrows\PSh(L(C))\colon\gamma^* \] induced by the localization functor \(\gamma\colon C\to L(C)\). Then, for any presheaf \(F\colon C\catop\to\An\) there is a canonical isomorphism of the form \[ \underset{w : W(x)\catop}{\colim} F(z_w\catop)\iso \gamma_!(F)(\gamma(x))\, . \]

Proof

We first construct the canonical comparison below. Both its source and target preserve small colimits in \(F\), so Yoneda density reduces its invertibility to the representable case, which is precisely Proposition 14.3.5.

For any presheaf \(G\) on \(L(C)\), the isomorphisms \(\gamma(s_w)\) induce an identification from the constant diagram with value \(G(\gamma(x))\) to the diagram \(w\mapsto G(\gamma\catop(z_w\catop))\). Since \(W(x)\catop\) is weakly contractible, the argument from Construction 14.3.3 shows that taking its colimit gives a natural equivalence \[ G(\gamma(x))\iso \colim_{w : W(x)\catop}G(\gamma\catop(z_w\catop)). \] Take \(G=\gamma_!(F)\). The unit \(F\to\gamma^*G\) also induces a map \[ \underset{w : W(x)\catop}{\colim}F(z_w\catop)\longrightarrow \underset{w : W(x)\catop}{\colim}G(\gamma\catop(z_w\catop)) \, . \] Composing with the inverse of the preceding isomorphism yields a canonical map \[ \underset{w : W(x)\catop}{\colim}F(z_w\catop) \longrightarrow \gamma_!(F)(\gamma(x)). \] Both sides preserve small colimits in \(F\). Therefore, to prove that this map is invertible, Corollary 12.6.4 tells us that it suffices to prove that it is invertible for \(F=\Hom_C(-,y)\) for some object \(y\) of \(C\). In that case, \(\gamma_!(F)\) is \(\Hom_{L(C)}(-,\gamma(y))\) by Proposition 12.3.18. The displayed map is then the canonical fraction map, which is invertible by Proposition 14.3.5.
