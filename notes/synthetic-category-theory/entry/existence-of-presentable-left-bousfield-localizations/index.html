Proposition 21.2.5. Let \(\kappa\) be a localizing subunivserse of \(\Cat\). If \(C\) is \(\kappa\)-presentable, then, for any small family \(S\) of morphisms of \(C\) between \(\kappa\)-small objects, the left Bousfield localization exists. Furthermore, the category \(L_S(C)\) of \(S\)-local objects is \(\kappa\)-presentable and the full embedding \[ L_S(C)\hookrightarrow C \] is a right adjoint that commutes with \(\kappa\)-filtered colimits.

Proof

Let \(C^\kappa\) be the full subcategory of \(\kappa\)-compact objects of \(C\). We may see \(S\) as a small family of morphisms in \(C^\kappa\), hence as a functor \[ \coprod_{f\colon S}\Delta^1\to C^\kappa\, . \] Recall that, for any small category \(C\), there is the free small category with \(\kappa\)-small colimits \(\langle C\rangle_\kappa\) associated to \(C\) by Theorem 17.3.3. Furthermore, since the subuniverse \(\kappa\) is localizing, the category \(\RexCat^\kappa\) of small categories with \(\kappa\)-small colimits has small colimits by Proposition 17.3.8.

The family of maps \(S\) thus defines functor preserving \(\kappa\)-small colimits of the form \[ \Big\langle \coprod_{f\colon S}\Delta^1\Big\rangle_\kappa\to C^\kappa\, . \] We can then form the pushout in \(\RexCat^\kappa\) of the form bellow. This commutative square remains a pushout in the category of large categories with \(\kappa\)-small colimits. We will prove that \[ L_S(C)=\Ind_\kappa(S^{-1}C^\kappa)\, . \] The functor \(\gamma\) induces an adjunction \[ \gamma_!\colon\PSh(C^\kappa)\rightleftarrows\PSh(S^{-1}C^\kappa)\colon \gamma^* \] with \(\gamma^*\) fully faithful that restrict to an adjunction of the form \[ \gamma_!\colon\Ind_\kappa(C^\kappa)\rightleftarrows\Ind_\kappa(S^{-1}C^\kappa)\colon \gamma^* \] by Proposition 17.2.21. Moreover, we have \[ \Ind_\kappa(C^\kappa)=C \] by Theorem 17.2.24 and Corollary 17.4.5. By construction, the full subcategory \(\Ind_\kappa(S^{-1}C^\kappa)\) is contained in the full subcategory \(L_S(C)\) of \(S\)-local objects. Conversely, any \(S\)-local object belongs to the essential image of \(\gamma^*\). Indeed, since \(C^\kappa\hookrightarrow C\) preserves \(\kappa\)-small colimits, given an \(S\)-local object \(x\) in \(C\), the restricted Hom functor \[ \Phi:=\Hom_C(-,x)\catop_{|_{C^\kappa}}\colon C^\kappa\to\An\catop \] preserves \(\kappa\)-small colimits and sends maps of \(S\) to isomorphisms, hence determines a commutative square of the form and thus a unique functor \[ \Psi\colon S^{-1}C^\kappa\to\An\catop \] such that \(\gamma\circ\Psi=\Phi\). In other words, if \(F=\Psi\catop\), we see that \(x\) is isomorphic to \(\gamma^*(F)\) through the identification of \(C\) with \(\Ind_\kappa(C^\kappa)\). We have thus proved that \[ \Ind_\kappa(S^{-1}C^\kappa)=L_S(C) \] so that the embedding \(L_S(C)\hookrightarrow C\) corresponds to \(\gamma^*\). The left adjoint of \(\gamma^*\) thus proves that the left Bousfield localization of \(C\) by \(S\) exists. The fact that \(S\)-local objects are stable under \(\kappa\)-filtered colimits can be checked directly or can be seen as a particular case of the last assertion of Proposition 17.2.21. Finally, Remark 17.2.27 proves that \(L_S(C)\) is \(\kappa\)-presentable.
