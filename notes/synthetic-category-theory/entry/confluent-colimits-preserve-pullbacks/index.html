Theorem 16.2.5 (Rezk, Sattler–Wärn). A small category \(A\) is confluent if and only if the colimit functor \[ \colim_A\colon\Fun(A,\An)\to\An \] commutes with pullbacks.

Proof

Assume that \(A\) is confluent. Under the unstraightening equivalence \[ \Un\colon\Fun(A,\An)\iso\LFib(A)\, , \] the colimit functor \(\colim_A\) corresponds to the functor \[ \LFib(A)\to\An \] defined by assigning to each left fibration \(p\colon X\to A\) the anima \(\Pi_{\infty}(X)\). In other words, it suffices to prove that, for any pullback square of small categories of the form in which all functors are left fibrations, and such that there is a left fibration \(p\colon Y\to A\), the induced commutative square is a pullback square. In order to prove this, we start by observing that, by virtue of Proposition 16.2.4, \(Y\) itself is confluent. For each morphism \(w\colon y_0\to y_1\) in \(Y\), the induced functor \[ w^*\colon Y_{y_1/}\to Y_{y_0/} \] is thus terminal. Since \(f\) is a left fibration, it is in particular proper. Therefore, the pullback squares show that \(w^*\) induces a terminal functor \(X_{y_1/}\to X_{y_0/}\). Since terminal functors are weak homotopy equivalences, the equivalence of properties (i) and (iii) in Quillen’s Theorem B (Theorem 13.4.5) implies that we have a canonical equivalence \[ \Pi_{\infty}(X')\iso \Pi_{\infty}(Y')\times_{\Pi_{\infty}(Y)}\Pi_{\infty}(X)\, , \] which proves that the functor \(\colim_A\) preserves pullbacks of animae.
Conversely, let us assume that the functor \(\colim_A\) preserves pullbacks in \(\An\). Observe that, for any pullback square of the form if we see \(\Pi_{\infty}(X)\) as an object of \(\An\), we have a canonical equivalence \[ \colim_A F=\Pi_{\infty}(X) \] in \(\An\). Given two morphisms \(u\colon x\to y\) and \(v\colon x\to z\) in \(A\), we thus have \[ \colim_{a : A}(\Hom_A(y,a)\times_{\Hom_A(x,a)}\Hom_A(z,a))=\Pi_{\infty}(A_{y/}\times_{A_{x/}}A_{z/})\, . \] Similarly we have: \[ \colim_{a : A}\Hom_A(y,a)\times_{\colim_{a : A}\Hom_A(x,a)}\colim_{a : A}\Hom_A(z,a) =\Pi_{\infty}(A_{y/})\times_{\Pi_{\infty}(A_{x/})}\Pi_{\infty}(A_{z/}) \] and since any category with an initial object is weakly contractible, this yields \[ \colim_{a : A}\Hom_A(y,a)\times_{\colim_{a : A}\Hom_A(x,a)}\colim_{a : A}\Hom_A(z,a)=e \] where \(e\) is the terminal anima. The property that \(\colim_A\) commutes with pullbacks of animae thus implies that \[ \Pi_{\infty}(A_{y/}\times_{A_{x/}}A_{z/})=e\, . \] As explained in Remark 16.2.2, this proves that \(A\) is confluent.
